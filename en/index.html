03How I work

Four decisions no client asked for, repeated across projects that never met each other.

01

Money is never a fractional number

Floating point loses cents, and a wrong cent in payroll is a labour dispute. I store amounts as integers and percentages as basis points, from the database to the screen.

Cents in Distribuição Financeira and Eventa Pro · pence as BigInt in GetMoto · basis points in Alcance

02

The past does not get rewritten

Changing a rule today cannot alter what already closed. The period result is frozen into an immutable snapshot, and cancelling posts an opposite entry instead of deleting the original.

Per-period snapshots in Saúde Brasil 360 and Distribuição Financeira · reversal entries in GetMoto · fee frozen at payment time in Eventa Pro

03

The server does not trust the client

A value arriving from outside is input, not truth. Totals are recomputed on the server at write time, and who can see what is resolved from the authenticated user — never from a request parameter.

Recalculation on import confirmation in Alcance · tenant scope in middleware in Atende Fácil · price rebuilt at checkout in Eventa Pro

04

The rule that cannot fail lives in the database

Under real concurrency, an application-level check loses. One seat, one open ticket per conversation, one live bot execution: the real guarantee is a transaction, a partial unique index and a trigger.

Hand-written partial indexes in Atende Fácil · seat hold inside a transaction in Eventa Pro · append-only history through a trigger
